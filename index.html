Use Cadence on your iPhone

No App Store version yet, but you can use Cadence right now in your browser and add it to your home screen like an app.

1

Open it in Safari

Go to app.cadencedating.ie on your iPhone and sign up or log in.

2

Tap the Share button

It's the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari.

3

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the menu, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Cadence appears with its own icon.

Open Cadence in your browser

The web version works with email and password and doesn't send push notifications. It also works on Android and desktop.

Questions

The short version — email us if you want more detail.

Is Cadence free?

Yes — join, build a profile, and swipe with a daily limit for free. Cadence Pro (€9.99/month) unlocks unlimited swipes, rewind, boost, seeing who liked you, and club/pace filters.

What areas is it available in?

Cadence is launching Dublin-first, with other Irish cities opening up as more runners join from each one.

How is this different from a normal dating app?

Matching is built around running — your pace, favourite distance, running club, and the race you're training for — not just photos and a bio. You can even search around a race you're training for instead of just near home.

Is my data safe?

Cadence is operated from Ireland and built to GDPR requirements. Your exact location is never shown to other users — only an approximate area. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

When can I actually download it?

Cadence is available now on Android from Google Play. On iPhone, use the web app and add it to your home screen (steps above); an App Store version is coming soon. Email us below if you would like to hear when it launches.
