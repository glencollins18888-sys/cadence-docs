Questions

The short version — email us if you want more detail.

Is Cadence free?

Yes — join, build a profile, browse runners, send run requests, chat and plan runs for free. Cadence Pro (€9.99/month) adds a longer search radius, club and pace filters, following extra races and hosting a weekly group run.

What areas is it available in?

Cadence is launching Dublin-first, with other Irish cities opening up as more runners join from each one.

How is this different from other running apps?

Most running apps track your runs, and most social apps connect you with anyone. Cadence connects you with runners who match how you run — your pace, favourite distance, running club and the race you're training for — and lets you plan a run together in the chat. You can even search around a race instead of just near home.

Is my data safe?

Cadence is operated from Ireland and built to GDPR requirements. Your exact location is never shown to other users — only an approximate area. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

When can I actually download it?

Cadence is in private testing right now — closed testing on Android, TestFlight review on iOS. Email us below for early access.
